THUMBYROGUE

An Endless Voxel Descent
A real-time isometric hack-n-slash roguelike for the Thumby Color · 128×128 · RP2350
This guide covers the loot / inventory and level-generation systems implemented so far.

Controls

D-pad move (screen-relative) A attack — swing / fire B jump LB / RB rotate view ±90° MENU inventory

Reach the teal down-stairs beacon to descend. Step on the gold merchant pad to shop. Walk onto chests to open them.

Loot & Inventory (Diablo-4-lite)

The hero has no fixed class — the equipped weapon defines your playstyle (melee / ranged / caster), and a full 6-slot paperdoll of gear shapes the rest of your build. Loot vacuums into a 15-slot backpack; open MENU to compare, equip, salvage and socket.

Economy — salvage & the merchant

Salvage unwanted gear (B in the inventory) into gold. The merchant pad (one per floor) opens a shop:

  • Buy — four rolled items at prices scaled by rarity & depth
  • Gamble — pay gold for a random item
  • Reroll — re-roll your equipped weapon's affixes
  • Upgrade — raise your equipped weapon's base damage

Level Generation

Every floor is carved fresh into a static 64×64×64 voxel space and rendered through a DDA raycaster at a fixed isometric tilt. The layout is BSP rooms + corridors with a guaranteed, flood-fill-verified path from the up-stairs to the down-stairs — so every permadeath floor is solvable.

Depth bands

The theme shifts every 4 floors — reskinning blocks, swapping the enemy roster, and ending on a champion mini-boss. After the last band the sequence loops while enemy stats keep climbing: an endless descent.

What's in a floor

Rooms & corridors
BSP partition; thin 1-block ruined walls with ~18% gaps, not solid mazes
Background terrain
rolling value-noise hills fill the open space beyond the rooms
Lava pits + bridges
glowing 1-deep trenches with a safe cross-bridge; burn on contact, jump out
Elevated pedestals
chests you must jump onto (height-gated to open)
Moving platforms
oscillating slabs that carry you across hazards
Braziers
lit-lamp blocks light ~2/3 of rooms via the lightmap
Spike traps
telegraphed floor spikes, contact damage scaling with depth
Chests & merchant
loot containers + one shop pad per floor
Up / down stairs
glowing beacons visible across the room for navigation

Enemies

Cuboid creatures with wander → chase → telegraphed wind-up → strike AI (every hit is fair — foes flash white-hot before they swing). Stats scale with depth.

Rat — fast, weak Slime — tanky hopper Skeleton — sturdy Spider — fast melee Champion — 3× HP, 1.7× size, boss floors

Tension systems

Light as a resource
torch fuel drains; in the dark, foes sense you farther & hit harder
Lava & falls
burning lava + fall damage from big drops
Permadeath
one life — gear & gold lost on death; depth is your score
