THUMBYROGUE

An Endless Voxel Descent
A real-time isometric hack-n-slash roguelike for the Thumby Color · 128×128 · RP2350
This guide covers the loot / inventory and level-generation systems implemented so far.

Controls

D-pad move (screen-relative) A attack — swing / fire B jump LB / RB rotate view ±90° MENU inventory

Reach the teal down-stairs beacon to descend. Step on the gold merchant pad to shop. Walk onto chests to open them. The fog-of-war minimap lives on the MENU screen (it was too big to leave up during play).

Floating damage numbers rise from every hit — green for damage you deal, red for damage you take.

Cheat hold LB + RB together for ~5s to open a level-skip menu (jump to any depth).

Loot & Inventory (Diablo-4-lite)

The hero has no fixed class — the equipped weapon defines your playstyle (melee / ranged / caster), and a full 6-slot paperdoll of gear shapes the rest of your build. Loot vacuums into a 21-slot backpack; open MENU to compare, equip, salvage and socket. The paperdoll cells show each equipped item's icon, your live stats sit beside them, and the fog-of-war map tucks into the top-right.

Rarity is deliberately stingy early so the descent feels like progression — at depth 1 a drop is only ~0.5% legendary / ~2.9% rare (mostly common & magic); by depth 20 that climbs to ~6% / ~18%. Affix magnitudes scale with both depth and rarity too.

Item detail page

Press B on any item (equipped or in the bag) to open its detail page — the full damage/armor, every affix, the legendary aspect, and its sockets. All item management lives here:

  • Equip / Unequip — or use the fast path: A on the grid equips/uses instantly
  • Socket a gem — opens a gem picker; choose a bag gem to drop into a free socket
  • Salvage — breaks the item down for gold, behind a yes/no confirmation so you never lose gear by accident

Sockets & Gems

Rare and legendary gear roll sockets. Gems drop into your backpack; open an item's detail page (B) and choose Socket a gem to slot one into a free hole.

Economy — salvage & the merchant

Salvage unwanted gear into gold from its detail page (B → Salvage → confirm). The merchant pad (one per floor) opens a shop:

  • Buy — four rolled items at prices scaled by rarity & depth
  • Gamble — pay gold for a random item
  • Reroll — re-roll your equipped weapon's affixes
  • Upgrade — raise your equipped weapon's base damage

Level Generation

Every floor is carved fresh into a static 64×64×64 voxel space and rendered through a DDA raycaster at a fixed isometric tilt. The layout is a BSP partition — each split carves a corridor between its two halves, so the room graph is always fully connected and the down-stairs is always reachable. Every permadeath floor is solvable.

Depth bands

The theme shifts every 4 floors — reskinning blocks, swapping the enemy roster, and ending on a champion mini-boss. Each band has a distinct identity and includes a ranged threat (archer or fire sprite) plus, where it fits, a flyer (bat); together the five bands use all 11 enemy types. After the last band the sequence loops while enemy stats keep climbing: an endless descent.

What's in a floor

Rooms & corridors
BSP partition; thin 1-block ruined walls with ~18% gaps, not solid mazes
Flagstone floors
4 scattered flagstone variants for texture variety; brick (cobble) walls
Background terrain
a low green grass lip rings the dungeon — a colour contrast, kept short so it never occludes the iso camera
Organic lava chasms
instant death on contact — no longer just square pits; carved as organic chasms spanned by a safe cross-bridge
Dank water
shallow murky-green pools you wade through (slowed) — translucent (the floor shows through) and a rippling animated surface, crypt water, not clear blue
Moving platforms
oscillating slabs — the only crossing to a marooned bonus-chest island over lava, not an optional shortcut
Elevated pedestals
chests you must jump onto (height-gated to open)
Torches
Minecraft-style wall/floor torches are the room light sources (lightmap BFS)
Spike traps
telegraphed floor spikes, contact damage scaling with depth
Chests & merchant
more loot containers per floor + one shop pad
Loot beams
dropped gear projects a vertical light beacon coloured by rarity (Diablo-style)
Up / down stairs
glowing beacons visible across the room for navigation

Enemies

Cuboid creatures with wander → chase → telegraphed wind-up → strike AI (every hit is fair — foes flash white-hot before they swing). Both stats and numbers scale with depth — the floor spawns 5 + depth enemies (up to 20) — and each type drops a different kind of loot, not just gold. Ranged foes fire projectiles and kite; flyers ignore the floor.

Tension systems

Light as a resource
a smooth torch glow that dims & shrinks as fuel drains, with wall-blocked shadows for real depth; out of fuel, foes sense you farther & hit harder
Lava = instant death
touching lava kills outright — chasms are crossed only by the bridge or a moving platform; falls still bruise
Permadeath
one life — gear & gold lost on death; depth is your score
Suspend & resume
an in-progress run auto-saves on descent (host file / device flash) and resumes on launch; wiped on death
